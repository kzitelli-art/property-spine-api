# Contracted Services portfolio population

Status: evidence-backed population rehearsal, not a production canonical write.

Research date: 2026-08-14

The rehearsal uses retained property files and June 2026 operating evidence for
Solo, Skyline, and Greenery. It proves what the first door can honestly contain.
The snapshots live only under `tests/support`; they are not runtime fixture truth.

## Solo - 4125 Chestnut

Canonical candidates supported by executed evidence:

- PrintWithMe resident print, scan, copy, and fax amenity. The completed 2021
  agreement states $199 per month, a one-year term beginning on installation,
  annual automatic renewal, a 30-to-90-day non-renewal window, and a 3.5 percent
  annual increase.
- Ehrlich pest control. The completed 2022 agreement states twice-monthly service,
  up to eight units per visit, $199.33 per month, and month-to-month continuation
  subject to 30 days written notice.

Evidence to reconcile:

- June 2026 accounting shows PrintWithMe at $234.60 and Patriot Pest Solutions at
  $356.40. The PrintWithMe observation stays separate from contract price. Patriot
  creates the question: did Patriot replace or supplement Ehrlich?
- Philly-wide Disposal at $1,510, Otis at $1,201.89, snow at $2,375 year to date,
  and mixed security/fire activity remain financial observations until their
  governing relationships are established.
- The 2020 Otis proposal and 2023 generator proposal are unsigned offers, not
  governing contracts. The historical Doyle and RentPath engagements have ended
  and do not belong in the first current register.
- The $151.98 line labelled landscaping purchased artificial greenery. It is not
  promoted into a landscaping service.

## Population gate

Production rows wait for an approved migration, approved shared integration, and
human confirmation against the complete retained artifacts. A GL vendor is never
auto-promoted into a governed provider. Evidence must enter through the sanctioned
source-artifact service when shared integration is coordinated.

The largest remaining gaps are the current agreements for observed providers,
renewal or termination outcomes, exact event dates for installation-anchored
terms, and a human-confirmed property-wide service census. The rehearsal now
surfaces those gaps instead of presenting an empty door or invented completeness.
