## Product ruling and intended experience

Human attention is scarce. Approving the same unchanged commercial terms repeatedly does not create useful judgment. The normal path should have two explicit commercial approval decisions:

1. **Author:** an authorized human approves the complete offer for one person, property and exact home/bed: rent, deposit, dates, fees, concessions and applicable conditions. The approval names the immutable offer version and its authority. It authorizes that commercial deal; it does not claim that an unseen application has been reviewed or approved.
2. **Execute:** after the actual application, required evidence and resident/guarantor signatures are available, the properly authorized human reviews the complete current package and, in one deliberate action, approves the application and signs for the company. Internally distinct facts must retain distinct canonical records, actual actor, authority and timestamps. Execution produces the existing pending tenancy outcome; it never asserts activation, payment, key handoff or possession.

Between those decisions, ordinary staff can handle inquiry/tour work, deliver an application through an authorized channel, collect missing information, prepare the exact authorized package and issue signing links under their actual permissions. Applicant acknowledgement and required signatures remain real acts. Do not re-enter or ask management to reconfirm identical authorized economics. Staff preparation, delivery, applicant work and exception handling do not become additional commercial approval ceremonies.

The two decisions need not be performed by the same person. This build does not grant Mike production powers or make Kameron a hardcoded dependency. An author may lack signing authority; a signer may lack pricing authority. Execute must have authority for every consequence it causes, including application approval and company signing. A signer-list entry alone does not invent application-approval authority. Configure synthetic actors deliberately for proof; actual staffing decisions remain QB/owner work.

An incomplete or conflicting application remains incomplete or conflicting. Authoring an offer must not approve a future applicant. The final Execute screen must present the actual application decision and its consequence, rather than silently approving behind a signing label. If a recorded property policy truly requires separate earlier judgment, show the exact exception and accountable human; do not invent screening thresholds, pretend screening happened, or silently ignore existing requirements. Unsupported exceptions may remain blocked without adding the old redundant confirmation steps to the ordinary path.
